# Automated-DCF
Automated DCF is a Python desktop app for Discounted Cash Flow valuation. It combines live ticker data, candlestick charts, Base/Bull/Bear presets, a full WACC builder, and Gordon Growth or Exit Multiple terminal values. Adjust any driver to recalculate implied share prices, generate instant valuation signals, and export models in one click.


My Automated DCF is a Python-based desktop application built to deliver precision valuation analytics for strategic insights. Designed to streamline institutional-style Discounted Cash Flow modelling, it brings live ticker data, historical candlestick charting, granular financial driver controls, and automated valuation signals together into a single dark-mode workspace. Instead of setting up complex spreadsheets from scratch for every company, analysts and investors can evaluate businesses and stress-test assumptions in seconds.

Getting started with a valuation requires just a few clicks from the top control bar. You can enter a stock ticker such as HCLTECH.NS, pick a scenario preset like Base or Bear, and select a preferred candlestick chart timeframe such as 6mo or 1y. Clicking Run DCF automatically loads the historical price chart alongside baseline model assumptions, calculates the implied share price against the current market price, and outputs a clear Buy, Overweight, Underweight, or Sell view complete with percentage upside or downside commentary.

Every assumption behind the valuation can be customised through the scrollable Model Drivers panel on the left side of the interface. Under Growth, you can set the forecast horizon—dynamically expanding or contracting individual year-by-year revenue growth inputs—and adjust the base revenue growth rate. Margins and capital efficiency are equally flexible, allowing you to fine-tune Gross Margin, EBIT Margin, Capex, Depreciation and Amortisation, and Net Working Capital as percentages of revenue to match your view of the business.

For discounting and long-term value estimation, the application includes a full WACC builder alongside dual terminal value options. You can configure the Risk-Free Rate, Equity Risk Premium, Beta, Pre-Tax Cost of Debt, Tax Rate, and Target Debt-to-Capital ratio directly in the panel. Depending on the sector and maturity of the company, you can switch seamlessly between the Gordon Growth method using a terminal growth rate and the Exit Multiple approach using an EV/EBITDA multiple.

To translate Enterprise Value into an accurate per-share figure, the Equity Value Bridge lets you review or override Net Debt, Minority Interest, Diluted Shares, and Anchor Revenue, while the Timing controls provide a custom Valuation Date and a Mid-Year Convention toggle. Once you adjust any driver, clicking Recalculate updates the implied share price, status summary, and valuation signal immediately without needing to reload market data, and the Export button lets you save your finished analysis with one click.

This tool is built strictly for educational, research, and analytical purposes, and its outputs depend heavily on user-defined assumptions and market data rather than serving as financial or investment advice.
